We are pleased to announce that three of our papers have been accepted to **IEEE ISMAR 2026**: two papers to the **IEEE TVCG Special Track** and one paper to the **Conference Track**.

## IEEE TVCG Special Track

**"Exploring the Effects of Olfactory Cues and Ventilation on Teleportation-based Navigation in VR"**  
Dongyun Han, H. Kim, S.-Y. Park, C.-G. Song, S.-J. Kim, and Isaac Cho

This paper investigates the effects of olfactory cues and ventilation on teleportation-based navigation in virtual reality.

**"Smelling the Way: Olfactory Modulation of Spatial Estimation and Path Integration in Virtual Reality"**  
S. Bak, Dongyun Han, S.-H. Kim, J. Kim, S.-J. Kim, and Isaac Cho

This paper investigates how olfactory cues influence spatial estimation and path integration during navigation in virtual reality.

## Conference Track

**"Investigating Scale-Control Strategies for Portal-Based Remote Object Manipulation in Virtual Reality"**  
S. Eun, Isaac Cho, and M. Lee

This paper investigates scale-control strategies for portal-based remote object manipulation in virtual reality.

The papers will be presented at **IEEE ISMAR 2026**.
